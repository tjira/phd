\chapter{Foundations and Numerical Studies}\label{chp:supporting_numerical_studies}
In academic research, a significant amount of work never makes it into peer-reviewed journals, yet it is absolutely essential for validating the methods used in published research. The motivation for this chapter is to present these vital, unpublished results. Here, we bridge the gap between abstract mathematical frameworks and practical execution by detailing the development and benchmarking of our custom-built quantum chemistry software package, \textsc{Zinq}.\supercite{10.5281/zenodo.18386143}

Implementing exact quantum dynamics reveals severe real-world algorithmic hurdles that are rarely discussed in the literature. This chapter details our unpublished solutions to these challenges, including managing exponential memory requirements, resolving gauge invariance issues, and mitigating boundary reflection artifacts. We then utilize this validated framework to perform an in-depth analysis of how effectively different \acrfull{tsh} algorithms capture quantum coherence. Together, these unpublished results provide a rigorous ground that guarantees the reliability of the published discoveries presented in Chapter~\ref{chp:primary_scientific_contributions}.
